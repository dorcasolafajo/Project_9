Delicious okro stew

Okra soup is one of the many vegetable soups that is widely eaten with swallow in Nigeria and other West African countries. Okra is either cooked plain or in a sauce with condiments and fish or meat . Plain okra needs a stew to make a complete accompaniment, while ila alasepo (Nigerian okra soup) is a complete soup.

cooking instructions for jollof Recipe

  1. wash the rice properly
  2. Blend the tomatoes and peppers ginger and garlic
  3. Fry the blended mixture with vegetable oil
  4. add the seasoning and water
  5. add the rice and cook soft for about 20 minutes on medium heat
  6. add butter and onions and leave about another 5 minutes on a high heat.
  7. mix together with your wooden spoon,and our yummy jollof is read to be served.

cooking instructions for okra

  1. Cook the washed beef, goat meat, and pre-soaked stockfish in a pot, Add the chopped half-onion, bouillon cubes, and salt. Pour in just enough water to cover the protein and boil on medium-high heat for about 30–45 minutes until tender. You want to end up with about 1.5 to 2 cups of rich, concentrated meat stock remaining in the pot
  2. While the meat boils, wash the okra and trim off the woody stems and tips. Finely chop the okra a knife or pulse briefly in a food processor so you have small, uniform pieces. Separately, blend the Scotch bonnet peppers and the remaining half-onion into a rough paste
  3. 5 minutes. Once the meat is tender, add the deboned smoked fish, ground crayfish, locust bean (if using), and the blended pepper-onion mix directly into the simmering meat stock. Stir well and let it boil for 5 minutes so the raw pepper flavor cooks out
  4. Do not cover the pot from this point onward. Pour in the red palm oil and allow it to dissolve into the simmering broth for 2 minutes. Stir in the chopped okra. Lower the heat to medium-low and simmer uncovered for 3 to 5 minutes, stirring frequently to release the "draw" without overcooking the okra into mush. (Tip: Covering the pot traps steam and breaks down the viscosity)
  5. 1-2 minutes, Stir in your sliced spinach, taste, and adjust salt if needed. Let it simmer for just 1 to 2 minutes until the greens wilt while staying bright green, then remove from the heat immediately. Serve hot alongside a swallow of your choice(popular pounded yam)
